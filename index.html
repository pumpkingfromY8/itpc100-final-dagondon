THAILAND · AUTHENTIC FLAVORS

Tom Yum Goong

A classic Thai soup renowned for its bold flavors and aromatic spices. This hot and sour soup features a tantalizing blend of lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves, combined with succulent shrimp.

◷ Preparation: 20 minutes ♨ Hot & Sour ✦ Thai Cuisine
“

The key to a delicious Tom Yum Goong lies in the balance of flavors – sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.

— Chef Somchai, Thai Cuisine Expert

Ingredients

Everything you need for this Thai classic.

  • Lemongrass 2 stalks
  • Thai Basil 1 cup
  • Kaffir Lime Leaves 3 leaves
  • Shrimp 500 g
  • Fish Sauce Quantity not specified
  • Lime Juice Quantity not specified
  • Chili Paste Quantity not specified
  • Galangal and Broth Quantities not specified

Preparation

Follow these steps to prepare your soup.

  1. 01
    Simmer the broth

    Simmer the broth for 10 minutes.

  2. 02
    Add the aromatics

    Add lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves.

  3. 03
    Cook the shrimp

    Stir in shrimp and cook until pink.

  4. 04
    Season the soup

    Season with fish sauce, lime juice, and chili paste.

  5. 05
    Garnish and serve

    Garnish with Thai basil and serve hot.

✦

Cooking Tips

  • Fish sauce adds authentic Thai flavor. The quantity was not specified in the recipe.
  • Chili paste gives the soup its signature heat. The quantity was not specified in the recipe.
  • Balance the sweet, sour, salty, and spicy flavors to suit your taste.
