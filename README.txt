TASTE OF TRAVEL — Website Project

Files:
- index.html: page structure and small interactive behaviors
- styles.css: responsive visual design

How to run:
1. Keep index.html and styles.css in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and the remote Unsplash food photographs.

Project content:
The supplied PDF describes "Taste of Travel" as an online platform celebrating global cuisine and culinary adventures, with immersive storytelling and recipes from around the world. Destination names, sample recipes, and visual copy in this website are creative demonstration content to make that brief into a complete website; they are not additional facts from the PDF.

Notes:
- Newsletter form is a front-end demo only; it does not send/store email addresses.
- Recipe links are presentation placeholders and can be connected to individual recipe pages later.
- Image URLs are remotely hosted Unsplash images.
